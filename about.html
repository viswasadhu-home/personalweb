The throughline

25+ years in a changing industry.

I have spent more than 25 years helping enterprise technology organizations evolve: from infrastructure foundations to globally coordinated programs, modern observability, and emerging generative AI capabilities.

Across each shift, the challenge stays human as much as technical: make complexity legible, give teams a dependable operating model, and help leaders make decisions with evidence. That perspective informs how I approach transformation, from defining service outcomes to coordinating implementation across regions.

Today, my work brings together global program management, telemetry and service-level thinking, and hands-on experimentation with AI assistants and workflow automation. I am especially interested in practical adoption: solutions that fit real processes, have clear boundaries, and leave teams more capable than before.

Based in Hyderabad, India, I work comfortably across time zones and organizational boundaries, connecting engineering detail to executive intent.
